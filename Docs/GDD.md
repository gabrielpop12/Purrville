# Purrville: Little Chaos

A cozy 2D colony city builder with procedural worlds, bipedal customizable cats, resource gathering, autonomous jobs, emergent stories and progression from a small village to a futuristic city.

## Core pillars
- Cozy presentation, meaningful chaos and emergent relationships.
- Procedural seeded maps with guaranteed starter resources (future milestone).
- Manual RTS-style orders and automatic priority-based jobs.
- Physical resources, storage, production chains and currency.
- Modular cat appearance, personality, needs, memories and generations.
- Research and city progression through multiple eras.

## Milestone 0.1 — World Foundation
- Seeded procedural terrain prototype.
- Orthographic camera movement and zoom.
- Temporary colored tiles; final art will be original.

## Future milestones
0.2 cats and movement; 0.3 resource gathering; 0.4 construction; 0.5 automation; 0.6 personality and social systems; 0.7 emergent stories.
